Punctuality of 85.5% in Lausanne, above previous days

On 25 October 2026, 85.5% of measured trips in Lausanne were on time (less than 3 minutes late). The average delay is 1.5 minutes, and 9 trips out of 10 are less than 3.6 minutes late. The day before, punctuality was 82.7% (+2.8 points). The average for the previous 4 days is 76.0% (+9.5 points).

By mode, the metro reaches 98.8% on time, the train 87.3% and the bus 85.2%. Over the whole day, TL line 8 is the least punctual: 67.8% on time, with 105 trips cancelled (3.7%). Next come lines 7 (73.6%) and 18 (75.7%). In total, 393 trips were cancelled (0.7%), including 30 for the train (3.0%).

The most difficult time is late afternoon: 74.7% on time at 16:00 (average delay of 2.3 minutes), 75.0% at 17:00 and 79.5% at 18:00.

The largest measured delay is 51.5 minutes, on TL line 3 at the stop Lausanne, gare, for a trip scheduled at 07:25. The least punctual stop of the day is Recordon, with 66.2% on time.
