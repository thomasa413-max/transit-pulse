Lausanne: 72.7% of departures on time, up from the previous day

On 28 October 2026, 72.7% of the departures measured in Lausanne are on time (less than 3 minutes late). The average delay is 2.4 minutes, and 9 departures out of 10 are less than 5.8 minutes late. The day before, the on-time share was 70.7%, and the average of the previous 7 days was 76.9%. Out of 68'138 scheduled departures, 66'305 were measured and 1'019 were cancelled (1.5%).

The train (94.3% on time) and the metro (90.1%) are ahead of the bus (71.8%). Among the lines, TL line 7 shows 52.4% on time, followed by lines 1 (53.1%, 313 cancelled departures) and 18 (55.5%). SBB's R1 (100.0%), LEB's RR20 (98.8%) and TL line 41 (98.4%) are the most punctual.

The most difficult hours are 16:00 (52.1% on time), 17:00 (52.5%) and 18:00 (55.6%). Among stops, Oiseaux (46.6%), Dapples (47.5%) and Lausanne, Druey (49.6%) are the least punctual over the day.

The largest delay recorded is 76.2 minutes, on TL line 16 at the Grande-Borde stop, for a departure scheduled at 11:47.
