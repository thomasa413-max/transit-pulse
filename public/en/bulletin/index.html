Lausanne: 72.7% of departures on time, up from the previous day

On 28 October 2026, 72.7% of the departures measured in Lausanne are on time (less than 3 minutes late). The average delay is 2.4 minutes, and 9 departures out of 10 are less than 5.8 minutes late. The day before, the on-time share was 70.7%, and the average of the previous 7 days was 76.9%. Out of 68'138 scheduled departures, 66'305 were measured and 1'019 were cancelled (1.5%).
