Punctuality at 75.1% in Lausanne, low point at 18:00

On 22 October 2026, 75.1% of services arrived on time (less than 3 minutes late), with an average delay of 2.1 minutes. The day before, punctuality was 75.3%. Out of 63'890 scheduled services, 62'783 were measured and 497 were cancelled (0.8%). 9 out of 10 services were less than 5.2 minutes late.

By mode, over the whole day, trains were on time 91.0% of the time, the metro 87.9% and buses 74.4%. Among TL lines, line 18 was the least punctual (56.8%), followed by lines 1 (58.1%) and 8 (62.5%). Line 13 was the most punctual (99.1%), ahead of line 41 (98.0%).

The 18:00 hour was the most difficult: 57.8% of services were on time, with an average delay of 3.4 minutes and 93 cancelled services. It was followed by 17:00 (60.4%) and 16:00 (67.2%).

The largest delays concerned line 1: 66.2 minutes at Stade Olympique (scheduled at 05:45) and 65.9 minutes at Casernes (scheduled at 05:47). Among stops, Lausanne, Cour est was the least punctual, with 48.7% of services on time.
