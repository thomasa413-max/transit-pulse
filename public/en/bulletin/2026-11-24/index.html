Lausanne: 72.3% of departures on time, 7.8 points below the previous day

On 24 November 2026, 72.3% of departures were on time (less than 3 minutes late), with 66'100 departures measured out of 67'847 scheduled. The average delay is 2.8 minutes; 9 departures out of 10 are less than 6.2 minutes late. The previous day, punctuality stood at 80.1%. The average of the previous 7 days is 71.9%, a difference of +0.4 point.

Over the full day, trains recorded 91.7% of departures on time and the metro 84.8%, compared with 71.5% for buses. Among the least punctual lines are lines 362 (56.5%) and 435 (57.1%) operated by PAG, followed by line 21 operated by TL (59.1%). The RR20 line of the LEB is the most punctual, at 98.7%.

The most difficult hours are 19:00 (42.7% on time, average delay of 7.8 minutes), 18:00 (47.5%) and 21:00 (57.8%).

Over the day, 1'097 scheduled departures were cancelled, or 1.6%. The largest delay recorded concerns TL line 18: 123.8 minutes at the Halle Sébeillon stop, for a departure scheduled at 18:26.
