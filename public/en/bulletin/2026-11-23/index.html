Lausanne: 80.1% of departures on time, more difficult day at 08:00

On 23 November 2026, 80.1% of the departures measured in Lausanne were on time (less than 3 minutes late). The average delay was 1.8 minutes; 9 departures out of 10 were less than 4.4 minutes late. The day before, punctuality stood at 82.1% (a difference of -2.0 points). The average of the previous 7 days is 71.1% (a difference of +9.0 points).

The metro shows 95.0% of departures on time, the train 85.7% and the bus 79.6%. Line 701 (MBC Auto) reaches 98.2% and line 41 (TL) 96.6%. At the other end, line 21 (TL) is at 62.1%, followed by S41 (SBB) at 62.5% and line 54 (TL) at 68.3%.

At 08:00, punctuality falls to 60.2%, with an average delay of 3.0 minutes. Next come 16:00 (65.6%) and 17:00 (71.4%). Among the stops, Pont Chauderon (55.8%) and Denantou (57.7%) are the least punctual.

Out of 68'085 scheduled departures, 683 were cancelled (1.0%). The largest delays reach 44.3 minutes on line 20 (TL) at Beaulieu-Jomini (scheduled departure at 08:50) and 43.7 minutes at St-Roch (08:52).
