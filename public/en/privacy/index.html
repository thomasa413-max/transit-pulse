Privacy

Privacy

  • Visit statistics: only daily totals are stored (page views, visitors, country, referring site, device type, language). No IP address, no identifier. Deleted after 400 days.
  • Technical cookies: tp_d (count a browser once a day, 24 h), tp_v (first visit, 1 year), lang (chosen language, 1 year), tp_h (bot check passed, 12 h). No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.
  • Do not track: if your browser sends "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control", the visit is not counted.
  • Comments: the name and message you enter, the date, the country inferred by Cloudflare and your IP address, deleted after 90 days. Likes are tied to a salted hash of the IP address, never the address itself.
  • Bot check: Cloudflare Turnstile, loaded only when you write a comment or like one.
  • Performance measurement: loading speed (Core Web Vitals) is sent anonymously, as daily totals.
  • 3D map: buildings, terrain and aerial photos are loaded from the servers of swisstopo (Federal Office of Topography, geo.admin.ch), which see your IP address like any web server. Weather goes through our server: Open-Meteo does not see your address.
  • Hosting: Cloudflare (site and database) and GitHub (code and public data).

Data: opentransportdata.swiss (actual times and disruptions of Swiss public transport). Figures and texts are generated automatically.

Personal proof of concept, not affiliated with SBB, tl or any other operator.
